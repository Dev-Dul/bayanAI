How BayanAI works

Three things happen to every article you paste.

Simplify the language

Dense academic English is broken into shorter, clearer ideas — while preserving the original concepts, terminology and academic meaning.

02

Difficult English concepts are explained in Hausa — your strongest language — connecting unfamiliar academic terms to words and expressions you already know.

The localized Hausa explanation is synthesized into natural audio, so you can listen, follow along, and reinforce the concept through sound.
